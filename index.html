Our Story

Inspired by Karbala

Super 72 is not merely a coaching centre — it is a movement. Named in honour of the 72 martyrs of Karbala, this centre embodies their spirit of sacrifice, discipline, and unwavering commitment to truth and education.

An initiative of Imamia Medics International, Aligarh, Super 72 provides talented students from across India with world-class residential coaching to achieve their dreams in medicine and engineering.

"Education is the continuation of sacrifice."— Dr. Wasi Jafri, Founder
